GROWTH STRATEGY

Long-Term Growth Vision

Forward Integration Strategy

The long-term growth strategy is to integrate forward into the industry by building a processing plant for the copper ore. This will facilitate value addition through upgrading of the ore to better grades. The company will be able to offer an upgraded product and processing the ore before transporting to export destinations. This approach will also contribute to the beneficiation policies adopted by progressive African countries.

Processing Plant Development

Our planned state-of-the-art copper processing facility represents a significant investment in Namibia's mining infrastructure. The plant will utilize modern flotation and smelting technologies to produce high-grade copper concentrate and cathodes suitable for international markets. By processing ore domestically, we reduce transportation costs, create additional employment opportunities, and retain more value within Namibia's economy. The facility is designed with future expansion in mind, allowing us to scale operations as our mining activities grow.

Value Addition & Beneficiation

Value addition through local processing aligns perfectly with African governments' beneficiation policies, which encourage mineral-rich nations to process raw materials domestically rather than exporting them in their raw form. By upgrading our copper ore from low-grade material to refined copper products, we significantly increase the economic value captured by Namibia. This strategy transforms us from a simple extraction company into an integrated mining and processing operation that contributes more substantially to the national GDP.

Market Expansion & Export Capabilities

With processed, high-grade copper products, Tema will be positioned to access premium markets across Europe, Asia, and North America. Our strategic location in Namibia provides excellent access to international shipping routes through the Port of Walvis Bay, one of Southern Africa's most efficient deep-water ports. We are establishing relationships with major copper consumers in manufacturing, electronics, and renewable energy sectors, ensuring stable demand for our products for years to come.

Technology & Innovation Investment

Innovation is at the heart of our growth strategy. We are investing in automation, artificial intelligence for ore sorting, and advanced metallurgical techniques that improve recovery rates and reduce waste. Our research and development initiatives include partnerships with mining technology companies and academic institutions to pioneer new methods of minerals (PSG), Copper extraction and processing that are more efficient and environmentally sustainable than traditional approaches.

Workforce Development & Training

As we grow, so must our workforce. Our growth strategy includes comprehensive training programs to develop local talent capable of operating and maintaining sophisticated mining and processing equipment. We partner with technical colleges and universities to create specialized curricula in mining engineering, metallurgy, and industrial safety. Through apprenticeships and mentorship programs, we're building a skilled workforce that will drive Namibia's mining sector forward for generations.

Sustainable Growth Metrics

Our growth is measured not just in production volumes and revenue, but in our positive impact on communities, environmental stewardship, and contribution to Namibia's economic development. We set clear targets for local employment, environmental performance, and community investment, ensuring that our expansion benefits all stakeholders. This balanced approach to growth ensures long-term viability and strengthens our social license to operate.
