SUSTAINABILITY OPERATIONS

Commitment to Sustainable Mining

Our Sustainability Philosophy

Tema Mining is committed to the preservation, growth and realization of long-term shareholder value in a responsible manner that delivers sustained, long-lasting benefits to local communities and stakeholders. Our approach to partner with local stakeholders in all our endeavours bears testimony to our community upliftment ethos. We have a team that possesses deep, proven expertise in efficient mine exploration, rapidly advancing and successfully delivering world class mineral projects in Africa.

Environmental Stewardship

Environmental protection is not an afterthought at Tema—it's integrated into every aspect of our operations from planning through closure. We implement comprehensive environmental management systems that monitor air quality, water usage, soil health, and biodiversity. Our mining methods are designed to minimize land disturbance, and we maintain active rehabilitation programs to restore mined areas to productive use. We've invested in renewable energy systems to power our operations, significantly reducing our carbon footprint compared to conventional mining operations.

Water Resource Management

In the arid landscapes of Northern Namibia, water is precious. Our operations employ closed-loop water recycling systems that recover and reuse up to 85% of water used in mining processes. We've implemented advanced tailings management techniques that minimize water loss through evaporation and seepage. Additionally, we monitor groundwater levels and quality continuously to ensure our activities do not negatively impact local water resources that communities depend on for drinking and agriculture.

Biodiversity Conservation

Before any mining activity begins, we conduct thorough biodiversity assessments to identify sensitive habitats and species. We work with conservation organizations to relocate endangered plants and animals when necessary and establish protected conservation areas that offset our operational footprint. Our team includes environmental specialists who monitor wildlife corridors and ensure our operations don't disrupt critical ecosystems. We've partnered with local universities to conduct ongoing research into the region's unique flora and fauna.

Community Partnership Programs

True sustainability requires strong community relationships. We engage local stakeholders in decision-making processes, ensuring that community voices shape our operational plans. Our preferential procurement policies prioritize local suppliers and contractors, keeping economic benefits within the region. We support local entrepreneurs through training and microloan programs, helping diversify the local economy beyond mining. Regular community forums provide transparent communication channels where concerns can be raised and addressed promptly.

Health & Safety Excellence

The health and safety of our workers and surrounding communities is paramount. We maintain rigorous safety protocols that exceed industry standards, with regular training and equipment upgrades to prevent accidents. Our occupational health programs include regular medical checkups, mental health support, and wellness initiatives. We've achieved industry-leading safety records through a culture that empowers every employee to stop work if they identify hazards. Zero harm is not just a slogan—it's our unwavering commitment.

Mine Closure & Legacy Planning

Responsible mining means planning for the end from the beginning. We maintain dedicated closure funds and develop progressive rehabilitation strategies that restore land to beneficial use as mining progresses. Our closure plans include transitioning employment opportunities to sustainable industries, ensuring communities aren't left stranded when mining operations conclude. We're exploring innovative post-mining land uses including renewable energy installations, agriculture, and eco-tourism that can provide long-term economic opportunities.

Reporting & Transparency

We publish annual sustainability reports that detail our environmental performance, social investments, and governance practices. These reports follow international standards including the Global Reporting Initiative (GRI) and are independently audited to ensure accuracy and credibility. We believe transparency builds trust, and we're committed to openly sharing both our successes and areas where we need to improve. Stakeholders can access our environmental monitoring data and track our progress toward sustainability goals in real-time through our online portal.
