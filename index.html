UNVEILING THE VALUE OF THE EARTH

OUR OPERATIONS

  • Exploration Planning

    Meepo, Mining & Exploration is currently mining copper ore in Opowu Northern Namibia and has already been awarded more exploration rights together with its partners in Northern Namibia. We have in addition identified claims and EPL's with a view of forming mutually beneficial partnerships to develop and explore these highly prospective copper mining targets in Namibia.

  • Growth strategy

    The long term growth strategy is to integrate forward into the industry by building a processing plant for the copper ore. This will facilitate value addition through upgrading of the ore to better grades. The company will be able to offer an upgraded product and processing the ore before transporting to export destinations. This approach will also contribute to the beneficiation policies adopted by progressive African countries.

  • Sustainability

    Meepo Mining is committed to the preservation, growth and realization of long-term shareholder value in a responsible manner that delivers sustained, long-lasting benefits to local communities and stakeholders. Our approach to partner with local stakeholders in all our endeavours bears testimony to our community upliftment ethos. We have a team that possesses deep, proven expertise in efficient mine exploration, rapidly advancing and successfully delivering world class mineral projects in Africa.

SOCIAL OUTREACH

Operating in remote villages and in largely underdeveloped areas, it is important for Meepo to contribute to the improvement of the lives of villagers. We will issue bursaries and provide onsite training for students in the various mining faculties at tertiary institutions. This initiative will help ensure students have opportunities to complete their practical's while gaining valuable experience (TBA). Meepo will drill water wells in communities we are working in and assist the community by constructing community health centers and providing transport for locals to these health facilities.
