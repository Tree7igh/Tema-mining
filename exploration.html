EXPLORATION PLANNING

Strategic Exploration Planning

Our Exploration Approach

Tema Mining & Exploration is currently mining copper ore in Opowu, Northern Namibia, and has already been awarded more exploration rights together with its partners in Northern Namibia. We have in addition identified claims and EPL's with a view of forming mutually beneficial partnerships to develop and explore these highly prospective copper mining targets in Namibia.

Current Operations in Northern Namibia

Our flagship operation in Opowu represents the culmination of years of geological surveys, environmental assessments, and community engagement. The site has proven to be one of the most promising copper deposits in the region, with estimated reserves that could sustain operations for decades to come. Our team of geologists and mining engineers work around the clock to ensure efficient extraction while maintaining the highest safety and environmental standards.

Future Exploration Rights & Partnerships

Beyond our current operations, Tema has secured multiple Exclusive Prospecting Licenses (EPLs) across Northern Namibia. These strategic acquisitions position us at the forefront of copper exploration in Southern Africa. We actively seek partnerships with local and international stakeholders who share our vision of sustainable, community-focused mining operations. Our partnership model emphasizes knowledge transfer, local employment, and shared economic benefits.

Geological Assessment & Technology

Using cutting-edge geological survey technology, including drone-based LIDAR systems, ground-penetrating radar, and advanced geochemical analysis, we identify high-potential mineral deposits with unprecedented accuracy. Our exploration team combines traditional fieldwork expertise with modern data analytics to minimize environmental impact while maximizing discovery potential. This integrated approach allows us to make informed decisions about which sites warrant further investigation and development.

Environmental & Social Considerations

Every exploration project begins with a comprehensive Environmental Impact Assessment (EIA) and extensive community consultation. We believe that successful mining operations must be built on a foundation of environmental stewardship and social responsibility. Our exploration planning includes baseline environmental studies, biodiversity assessments, and water resource evaluations to ensure that our activities do not harm the delicate ecosystems of Northern Namibia.

Investment in Local Communities

As we expand our exploration activities, we prioritize hiring and training local community members. Our exploration teams include geologists, technicians, and support staff from nearby villages, providing valuable employment opportunities and skills development. We will also engage with traditional leaders and community representatives to ensure our operations align with local values and contribute meaningfully to regional development goals.
